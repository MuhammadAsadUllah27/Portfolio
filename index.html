ABOUT

About Me

I'm Muhammad Asad Ullah, a Computer Science undergraduate with a deep passion for robotics — specifically the intersection of humanoid systems, medical robotics, and artificial intelligence.

I'm drawn to robotics not just as an engineering discipline but as a way to meaningfully extend human capability — whether that's a surgeon's reach, a patient's recovery, or a researcher's ability to test ideas safely in simulation before deployment in the real world.

My technical foundation spans C++, Python, and ROS 2, with growing expertise in reinforcement learning, computer vision, and embedded systems. I believe great robotics work lives at the junction of low-level hardware control and high-level intelligence, and I'm deliberately building depth on both ends.

I'm always open to connecting with researchers, collaborators, and anyone who believes robotics can fundamentally change how we care for each other.
